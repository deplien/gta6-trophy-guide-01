An offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy carries its official name, description and grade as PlayStation publishes them, a guide in our own words that cites its sources, and a missable flag where the official description implies one. The app adds a staged roadmap to the platinum, a checklist whose progress is saved in the browser (localStorage) and can be exported and imported, filters for story, missable, collectible, activity and online trophies, and a spoiler shield that hides story trophy names and descriptions until they are earned. It runs entirely from its own files under app/ and makes no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
