    Roadmap to the Platinum — the order to earn them in

    Eight stages, built from the official trophy descriptions and the launch guides. Play the story first (Stages 1–4), do the one missable thing in Chapter 1, then clean up in free roam. PowerPyx estimates roughly 200 hours for the Platinum (60–80 story, ~100 checklist, ~10 cleanup) at a difficulty of about 4/10, in a single playthrough. Two golden rules: never use cheats on a trophy save (GTABase and TechWiser report they disable trophies and saving), and there are no online trophies — everything is single-player.

    Missables & warnings — read this before Chapter 1 ends

    ⚠ Also handle with care

    • Stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone): one report (TechWiser, via EGamers) says stranger chains can have windows tied to story progress. Not confirmed as missable — but start each chain when it first appears instead of banking all of them for the endgame.
    • Maximum Withdrawal (bank vaults) needs the optional Chapter 4 mission “That’s Where The Money Is…”. Take it when it appears in Chapter 4 so vaults open up.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving (GTABase & TechWiser). Keep this save cheat-free.
    • A Long Road (100%) is the long pole: its checklist only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats.
